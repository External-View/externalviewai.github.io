Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What is Inquiry Desk?

Inquiry Desk is a system for investor relations and impact teams to capture ESG investor inquiries, coordinate replies with a clear owner and deadline, and report on the volume and topics driving investor questions over time.

How do inquiries get into Inquiry Desk?

Forward the investor email to Inquiry Desk. AI extracts the request, organization, contact, topics, and reply deadline, into a new inquiry for your team to review and assign.

Does it replace our inbox?

It replaces the record, not the email itself. The inquiry lands against the organization's full history, tier, AUM, shareholder rank, past inquiries, calls, and meetings, instead of a message with no context.

Can it tell us why an investor is asking?

Yes. The AI Knowledge Assistant gives a sourced view of what's likely driving a specific inquiry, so your reply can speak to the real concern, not just the literal question.

What kind of reporting does it produce?

Inquiry status and volume, topic distribution by ESG theme, and monthly trend charts, exportable to PowerPoint or Excel for leadership.

Who deploys and supports it?

Inquiry Desk includes concierge onboarding, SSO and mobile device management integration, and 24/7 global support for enterprise teams.
